# I Tested a Private Payment From a Wallet With Zero Public RLS

**Independent Rayls Enygma Public testnet research, 21 November 2026.**

A fresh passkey wallet @devil was able to claim a shielded invitation, register its username without publicly funding its EVM account, and submit a **3 RLS private send**. The recipient research wallet @rq3dev26 independently displayed **3 shielded RLS** after the transfer. The public vault receipt confirms the encrypted-note settlement without revealing its private amount or recipient.
