## Critical evaluator distinction

**Verified:** two different funding routes each completed a real 3 RLS private send to the same controlled receiving wallet; the first newcomer stayed publicly unfunded, the second did not. Onchain encrypted note envelopes and public fee routing were independently reconstructed.

**Not verified:** general privacy advantage, statistical anonymity, direct shielded faucet before public funding, privileged auditor decryption, cryptographically unique treasury correlation, mainnet behavior, 100% reliability or an official guaranteed 10/10 RP score.

The evidence should be evaluated as independent, reproducible, limited-scope experimental research, not a generalized security audit or a marketing restatement.
